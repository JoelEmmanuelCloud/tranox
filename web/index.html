TTranox

Pay freelancers abroad in the money they spend.

The client pays a milestone in dollars. Tranox converts it on-chain at a firm quote and sends the freelancer naira, rupiah, pesos or reais in their phone wallet, minutes after the work is approved.

cNGN · Nigeria IDRX · Indonesia wARS · Argentina wBRL · Brazil wMXN · Mexico wCOP · Colombia

How it works

1
The client pays

From the job page, the client pays each milestone in USD₮ from their own wallet, such as MiniPay.

2
The client approves

When the work is done, the client approves the milestone by signing with the same wallet. Signing is free.

3
The freelancer is paid

The Tranox agent checks the money arrived, converts it on Textile FX, and sends the local stablecoin to the freelancer.

Why it is safer

No payout without the money

The agent pays a milestone only after the client's USD₮ has arrived on-chain.

Only confirmed wallets

Freelancer wallets are confirmed with each freelancer before any payout can reach them.

Public receipts

Every conversion and payment is a Celo transaction anyone can check.
