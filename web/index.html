How it works

Three steps from a dollar invoice to money you can spend

No bank wire, no payout platform, no second conversion. Each step is a Celo transaction anyone can check.

STEP 1

The client pays

From the job page, the client pays each milestone in USD₮ from their own wallet, such as MiniPay.

STEP 2

The client approves

When the work is done, the client approves by signing with the same wallet. Signing is free and moves no money.

STEP 3

You get paid locally

The Tranox agent confirms the money arrived, converts it on Textile FX, and sends your local stablecoin straight to you.

Built to be safe

Nobody gets paid by mistake

No payout without the money

The agent pays a milestone only after the client's USD₮ for it has arrived on-chain.

Only the client can approve

Approval needs a signature from the client wallet on the job. A shared link cannot release money.

Only confirmed wallets

Each freelancer's wallet is confirmed with them before any payout can reach it, with daily spending limits on top.
